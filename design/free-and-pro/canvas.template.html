Changes needed across the app

01

Replace the broad Pro switch

Separate monitoring, publishing, claim processing and authorised follower reading. A follower's entitlement must never decide whether they can read.

02

Make following free end to end

Remove the three-person subscription cap from server invitations and acceptance, screens and notifications. Preserve privacy, blocks and abuse controls.

03

Split saving from monitoring

Keep basic flight lookup/import and manual entry free. Gate fresh and cached live data, foreground/background polling, push updates and own live activities on the server.

04

Power family updates from the traveller

Reuse the traveller's monitored flight for approved viewers. Do not create a paid provider request for every parent or grandparent.

05

Protect publishing and preserve drafts

Check author Pro at publication. Keep private photo saving free; retain text and image selections across paywall exits, purchases and upload retries.

06

Explain the benefit on this trip

Show the large introduction once per account; then use the same compact live-updates entry on home and in trip details. Closing it on home hides it there permanently. Show live coverage, missing features, access dates and unavailable data honestly. Never present old or sample gate details as live.

07

Make cancellation ordinary

Keep access until expiry and show the date. Then stop new paid work and automation, retain existing claim documents and history, and allow free family reading of existing permitted content.

08

Update the whole offer

Change the remote paywall, Settings, onboarding, invite landing pages, website, store listings and support copy. Lead with travel, not claims or family-size restrictions.

09

Give reminders a clear limit

Explicit opt-in only, one reminder and no unsolicited near-departure nudge after the introduction. Cancel/reschedule when the trip changes or the person subscribes.

10

Plan the transition

Live facts and posting are not gated this way today. Agree treatment of existing free users and active flights. Preserve all purchases; resolve the Galaxy build's billing exception.

Three product decisions
before implementation.

Eligibility: claim preparation stays Pro. Manual checks are recommended free; the Claims panel also previews gating every new check.

Postcards: keep the current flight-day to next-day publishing window initially, or expand to the whole holiday. Explain eligibility before checkout.

Transition: decide existing-user and in-flight grace. Never stop a family's updates mid-trip without an explicit policy.
