Where does “trip readiness” fit?

It is a proposed explanation.
It is not a screen in the app today.

I used “trip readiness” as a design label. A clearer name is “What Pro adds to this trip.” In this proposed flow, it is a sheet that opens only after the traveller taps “See Pro for this trip.” Saving a flight does not open it.

Latest refinement: one large introduction per account, then a home status card with permanent dismissal. Trip details keep the same “Live updates off” entry. The Pro offer is shorter, with a single “Remind me 2 days before this trip” button before its detailed confirmation. Try the updated timing and reminder flow. This page preserves the earlier explanation of the save → sheet → plans sequence. Replace the aircraft upsell with the live-updates card in trip details. Claim preparation stays Pro. When would we show it?

For a free traveller's own upcoming flight, after an explicit tap on the one-time introduction or compact “See Pro” row. The existing “Where’s your plane?” entry opens an inbound-focused Pro offer directly; a claim action opens a claim-focused offer. It can be opened immediately after saving or later, closer to travel. It does not open automatically at a countdown, when logging a past flight, when reading someone else's trip, or for an active subscriber.

Do we need a separate explanation step?

It is optional. This canvas makes the earlier proposal explicit. If this feels like one screen too many, the same “Saved / Pro adds” comparison can sit above the plans in the existing paywall: saved trip → combined explanation and plans → store confirmation. That would remove step 3 as a separate sheet.

Design artifacts only. Existing screens are simplified reconstructions, not exact screenshots. The explanation sheet, Pro card and wording are proposed. Prices are illustrative (€4.99 monthly / €29.99 yearly); use actual store offers in an implementation.
