The next step for grouped trips

Keep the live flight close.
Keep its place in the trip clear.

The current card still takes a flight out of its group. Here is what that does now, and two ways to keep the trip easy to follow.

00 / Current behavior As implemented

Moves out of the list.

The live card is at the top. The original flight row is omitted.

What gets lost: the flight’s place in the trip. A stay or connection can lose the flight beside it.

A / Keep a small pointer Recommended

Live above. Place kept.

Keep the live card. Give its compact route row the same moving border.

Smallest practical change. The route appears twice, but the countdown, gate and boarding pass appear only in the live card.

B / Expand in its group Alternative

The flight stays put.

Turn the existing row into the live card. Offer a shortcut from the top.

One flight, one place. Later flights can be well below the fold. It takes a tap or a scroll to see the gate.

Why I would choose A

“Today” and “my whole trip” remain easy to find.The card stays where travellers already expect it. The compact route row explains where that flight belongs. Both sizes use the same clockwise border animation, so the link is visible at a glance.

Name the group on the live card.YYZ → BOS says “Canada trip”. BOS → HEL says “US trip continued”. Tapping the group name goes to its place in the list; the small row goes back to the card.

Keep the whole trip under “Current trip”.Determine this before taking out the live row. Between flights it is still the current trip. After the final travel-day window finishes, file the complete trip under its year.

Keep the approved grouping exactly as it is.Country flags and dates on the right. Short stay accents. Dotted connections. US, Canada and US continued stay flat, without separators between them.

What changes as you travel

01 / BeforeNormal flight card

Flights stay in travel order. The reminder can start 24 hours before departure, using “Travel card above” rather than “Live”.

02 / Travel daySmall route pointer

The live card holds the details. The compact row shares its moving border, colour and rhythm. Connecting flights keep their link as the card hands over.

03 / AfterwardsNormal card returns

Restore the flight in place after its arrival window. Keep the remaining trip together. At home, the whole trip moves to history.

One flight saved? Put its US heading on the live card and omit the redundant pointer. Adding a return or Canada flights creates the appropriate full list automatically. Unknown dates never produce a stay count.

A delayed flight? Gate changes and the delay remain on the existing live card. The compact border follows the same status colour; the pointer never runs an independent countdown. Flights without live tracking keep ordinary cards.

While someone is reading: preserve their scroll position when a flight becomes live or hands over. Only a tap on a shortcut should jump the list. With Reduce Motion enabled, both borders stay still.
