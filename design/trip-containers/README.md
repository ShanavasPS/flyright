# Trip container canvas

Design review, 23 September 2026. Option C, including the revised header gap,
16 px corners and 12 px between groups, is now implemented in the Flights list.
See `docs/trip-grouping.md` for the app behavior and validation. This canvas
preserves the design comparison; “Current” means the layout before containers.

Open `canvas.html` in a browser. It is self-contained and works offline. Choose an example, switch appearance, scroll each phone, or enable **Compare current**. The live-card links scroll within their own preview. Feedback downloads a text file; nothing is transmitted.

## Three options

| Option | Fill | Border | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A · Soft surface | Field | Hairline, 1 px | Clear grouping with little added visual weight. |
| B · Clear outline | Selected | Silver in light; secondary in dark, 1 px | The clearest boundary for travellers who find the current list hard to scan. |
| C · Title band | Field, with selected behind the header | Hairline, 1 px | Selected by the user; revised with more header spacing and smaller corners. |

Each enclosing surface includes the flag, title, date range, flights, connections and stays. The proposed full-width trip separators are removed. The same surface treatment applies to every flat destination group, including US trip continued; there is no nested container around an overall itinerary.

Option C now has a 16 px outer radius, reduced from 32 px. Its title band has 12 px vertical padding and a 12 px gap before the first flight or stay. The band grows with its text, including wrapped titles. A and B retain their original 32 px corners and header spacing.

All options retain an 8 px content inset. Option C now has 12 px between containers, a small increase from 8 px; A and B keep 8 px. The container extends into the existing screen margins so flight-card width is preserved. Inner row spacing remains 4 px. Flight cards retain their existing sheen, 24 px corners and compact spacing. Stay accents remain 16 px, while connections keep their dotted vertical stem and clock.

## Scenarios and current behavior

Eight fictional examples: USA → Canada → USA; simple return; connecting return; two independent trips; first flight live; later flight live; a single saved flight; completed trip.

The fixtures read the current pure presentation functions in `src/services/trip-groups.ts` at commit `1860045`. They do not open a database or modify traveller data. Dates, grouping, connections, stay lengths and the active flight's position are generated by those functions.

- YYZ → BOS belongs to Canada. US trip continued starts before the resumed US stay and contains BOS → HEL.
- A first live flight expands inside its group below the small travel summary, with no duplicate row or jump links.
- A later live flight stays at the top and retains its full original row. “View in trip ↓” and “View live card ↑” connect them.
- The active flight keeps its 1.5 px animated border: a 30% highlight makes a clockwise lap in 3.2 seconds. The enclosing group border stays still. Reduce Motion stops the sweep.

The previews reconstruct the current app rather than embed native screenshots. Airline chips use the existing plane fallback; system navigation materials are simplified. Those shared details are identical across A, B and C. The current-layout comparison retains the existing independent-trip separator.

## Design sources

Read the cached FlyRight design-system export: `project/README.md` and `project/tokens.json`, synced 20 September 2026 from `main@02c3dbd`. The public artifact was unavailable during this review and the cached export contained no component READMEs. Current app component sources supplied the trip heading, stays, connections, flight rows and live-card structure. The user's requested fill and border are the intentional extension to the brand's usual white surfaces.

Light field `#F2F5F9`, selected `#E8EEF6`, hairline `#E3E8EF`, silver `#A9B8CE`. Dark field `#101D34`, selected/hairline `#1B2C4A`, secondary `#5A6A7E` for B's stronger outline. Cobalt is reserved for existing actions and the live border, rather than assigned to ordinary groups.

## Files and sharing

- `canvas.html`: interactive offline review.
- `overview.png` / `overview-dark.png`: static three-option comparisons.
- `trip-containers.canvas`: JSON Canvas board, with image paths relative to the repository root.
- `previews/`: scenario and individual-option captures.
- `review.json`: canvas verification results; not app or native test results.
- `FlyRight-trip-containers-review.zip`: standalone review package; its Canvas image paths are adapted for the extracted folder.

Send the ZIP, ask the reviewer to extract it and open `canvas.html`. A single copy of `canvas.html` also works without the supplemental screenshots and notes.

## Rebuilding this artifact

Run `node design/trip-containers/build.mjs`. To regenerate screenshots and canvas checks, start isolated Chrome with remote debugging on port 9443, then run `node design/trip-containers/render.mjs`. These scripts write only this design directory. They do not change app code, app data, builds or releases.
