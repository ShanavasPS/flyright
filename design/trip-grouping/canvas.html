The days between flights.

Keep the flight cards and destination headings. Compact connection labels link flight legs; short lines frame each stay label; full-width separators appear only between independent trips. These are three examples of the same design.

List detail below the existing travel-day area. No new trip cards, nested trips or segment tabs. The existing navigation stays in place.

02 / Connecting flights

After the final leg.

Compact connections. A clear break for the stay.

Two different visual cues. A small clock and dotted vertical line connect flight legs. A bed icon and “Stay” label with short side lines mark time at the destination. Full-width lines separate independent trips only.

03 / US → Canada → US continued

Three separate groups.

US trip, Canada trip, then US trip continued. All at the same level.

Canada includes the return to Boston. LGA → YYZ and YYZ → BOS stay together, with 4 days in Canada between them. The next header is US trip continued, followed by 9 days in the US and the flight home, BOS → HEL.

Pair only adjacent returns

When nothing comes between the outward and return journeys, keep them together. An intervening destination starts a separate group.

Count the days on the ground
