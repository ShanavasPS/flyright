## How to edit this file

- Change the words after `SAY:` freely. Keep each line inside its **budget** —
  narration runs at about **2.6 words per second**, and the renderer needs
  0.85s of air per beat, so a 4s beat holds ~9 words and a 10s beat ~24.
  If a line has to be longer, raise the beat's duration instead and I will
  re-balance the rest.
- Change `IN`/`OUT` to move a beat's footage. Times are `m:ss.s`.
- Write `DROP` on a beat's first line to remove it, or add a new beat block
  anywhere using the same shape.
- `ON SCREEN:` is my description of the footage, not narration — it is there so
  you can check I picked the right seconds. Correct it if I read it wrong.
- Anything under **Notes for Claude** at the bottom comes back to me.

## Ground rules already applied

- No "advocate when a flight goes wrong". FlyRight works out what the airline
  owes and helps raise the claim; the app generates documents you send yourself.
- The opening says what the app is now: a travel buddy during the trip and a
  journal and history after it — one place for all of it.
- Nothing claims automatic filing, a guaranteed payout, or a cut of the money.
