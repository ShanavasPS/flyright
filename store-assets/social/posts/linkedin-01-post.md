Fifteen years into building apps, I finally shipped one of my own: **FlyRight** — built with Expo and entered into RevenueCat’s Shipaton.

There was always a reason.

No designer. No backend person. No one to handle the fifty small things between an idea and an app people can actually download.

AI removed the blocks.

Every “I can’t do that part alone” became “let’s do it together this afternoon.”

FlyRight is an app for the day you fly.

Add a flight by number, boarding pass or airline PDF, and it stays with you: check-in, gate, boarding, layover, landing. A live card on your lock screen. The people who care can follow along, so nobody has to text “did you land?”

Every trip becomes a journal with photos and notes, and a world map of everywhere you’ve flown.

And when a flight goes wrong, FlyRight already knows you’re on it. The delay notification tells you what the airline owes you under EU261, and you can claim right there.

**Trackers tell you it’s late.**
**FlyRight tells you what you’re owed.**

Live on the App Store and Google Play. Free, no account needed to start. Link in the comments.
