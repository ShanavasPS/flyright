My wife has never taken a flight on her own. So I imagined what her first solo trip would look like, and how FlyRight could help her through every step of it.

The video below is that trip, Helsinki to Tokyo, and she stars in it. I hope that after watching it, she will want to fly solo to Japan.

There's also a short cameo from me and our daughter, who was sad that she wasn't in the video. It is her mom's big trip, after all.

FlyRight was made to be personal. As Martin Scorsese said, "The most personal is the most creative." It keeps her company through the whole day: when to leave for the airport, the gate and boarding time on her Lock Screen, her flight on a live globe, and the people at home following along without having to ask "have you landed yet?"

The film scenes were generated from our family photos; every screen in the video is the real app.

I'm proud to share FlyRight with everyone. It's free on the App Store and Google Play, and any feedback or suggestions are always welcome. There's a lot more I want to build to make travel easier.
