FlyRight 1.0.37 redraws the Travel stats screen and the Add Flight flow.

Travel stats (My travels → the navy summary card → "All travel stats") now shows a passport-style summary, the longest flight drawn on the app's own globe, the top destination in its country's flag colours, the most-flown airline in the colours of its logo, and the most-flown aircraft type. Each card opens a list: every flight, every place by country, every airline, every aircraft type, and an aircraft type opens the flights flown on it. Airline logos are loaded from a public image CDN, flag images from flagcdn.com; both are read only to pick the card colour. No new permission is requested.

Add Flight (the + on My travels) is now a sequence of screens inside My travels with the standard back button: flight number → date → details → result. The first screen offers finding a flight by number (with Scan and Upload) or adding a trip by hand. Choosing an airline for a hand-entered trip opens a searchable list of every airline.

Reviewing Travel stats requires trips in the journal. Sign in with the configured reviewer account: enter the demo email, then choose Use another method and Sign in with your password. The existing demo credentials are unchanged, and that account already holds trips. Alternatively add a flight manually from My travels. The aircraft section appears only for flights found by their number, once the airline has assigned an aircraft.

No purchase is required for any of this. Existing account and trip data are retained on update; the local database gains two columns for the aircraft type, migrated automatically on first launch.
