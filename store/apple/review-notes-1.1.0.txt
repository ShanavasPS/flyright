FlyRight 1.1.0 reorganises the app's navigation. Nothing is removed; the same screens are reached differently.

New Home tab. The app now opens on Home, which shows what is happening now: the traveller's own travel day as a live card, a row of faces for the people they follow who are currently flying, and those people's trip photos and notes. Home is empty for a new account, and then shows what would fill it — a flight of their own, or someone to follow.

Flights (the first tab in 1.0.39) keeps the journal: every flight taken, the all-time summary card, and the + button to add one. A flight that is in the air appears under a "Live" heading with a countdown to the landing; previously today's flight was hidden from this list because the live card stood in for it.

Friends shows the circle: who follows the traveller, who they follow, invitations, and a live card for anyone currently flying.

Settings is no longer a tab. It is reached from the profile picture in Home's top-right corner and is titled "Profile"; every screen inside it is unchanged, and existing links to it still work.

Fix: a traveller who signed up with an email address and never entered a name had no searchable profile, so other people could not find or follow them. Those accounts now work.

To review: sign in with the configured reviewer account (enter the demo email, then choose Use another method and Sign in with your password). The app opens on Home. Tap Flights for the journal and the + button, Friends for the circle, and the picture in Home's top-right corner for Settings. Adding a flight by hand with a departure a few hours from now shows the live card on Home and the live row on Flights. No purchase is required. Existing account and trip data are retained on update.
