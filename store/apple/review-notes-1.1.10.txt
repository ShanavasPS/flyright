What's new to review:

Changes to how the Flights tab lists trips. Nothing about purchases, permissions or what Free and Pro include changes.

1. Sort chips. Beside the "Upcoming" heading a small chip switches between "Next up" (soonest departure first) and "Recently added". Beside the first year heading of past trips a chip switches between "Latest" and "Oldest". The choice is remembered on the device.
2. Past trips read newest first, including the flights inside each trip (the flight home is on top).
3. "Current trip" shows only the destination being travelled; destinations already flown appear with past trips.
4. The all-time summary card at the top of Flights opens Travel stats from its right edge (chevron).

To see them: sign in with the review account, open Flights and scroll to "Upcoming" and the year headings. The review account already has trips.

The existing review account and credentials remain valid. Use the email sign-in form and choose the password option if prompted for another method.
