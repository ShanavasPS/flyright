This update changes Free/Pro access and introduces a trip-specific Pro offer. Saving past/future flights and private journal photos, following people, and viewing permitted postcards remain free. Pro covers monitoring the traveller's own flight, publishing postcards and preparing new claim documents. A free follower can receive updates shared by a Pro traveller. Existing claim records remain readable; manual historical delay eligibility checks remain free.

The offer appears only with a future flight. The first home introduction becomes a compact card; its close control permanently hides it from home, while future trip details retain the entry. The optional reminder is explicitly confirmed and appears in-app two days before departure; it is not a deferred purchase or scheduled push.

Guests can browse actual store plans, then must sign in before purchase or restore. Successful signup/sign-in returns to the selected plan; the user must explicitly continue again to initiate StoreKit. Continue free never purchases. Subscription prices, periods, renewal information, restore and legal links are available on the plans screen.

The existing review account and credentials remain valid. Use the email sign-in form and choose the password option if prompted for another method. No purchase is required to review the free journal, map, following, existing claims or Pro offer/plan screens. New paid features require an active Pro entitlement.

Also included, all display-only: each trip heading now carries the destination country's flag faded across it, with the dates under the city name; past trips are ordered newest first, including the destinations within one trip; a distant flight is counted in weeks, months or years rather than hundreds of days; a followed person's trips use the same destination grouping as the traveller's own journal; and a flight saved without a valid arrival time no longer splits its trip apart. Also city-based trip headings, connected-flight grouping and duplicate/codeshare boarding-pass matching. No new device permissions are introduced.
