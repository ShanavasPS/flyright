What's new to review:

A bug fix. On 1.1.8 the live flight card on the Flights tab drew its main fact (the gate, check-in desk, seat or baggage belt) and, after landing, the arrival city only a few points tall. They now render at full size again. Nothing else changes.

To see it, open Flights while a flight in the review account's journal is within a day of departure, or in the air: the card at the top shows the gate or seat in large type on the right.

No new device permissions, no change to what Free and Pro include, and no change to purchases.

The existing review account and credentials remain valid. Use the email sign-in form and choose the password option if prompted for another method. The review account already has trips.
