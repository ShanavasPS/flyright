What's new to review in 1.2.3:

Nothing about purchases, prices or the existing Free and Pro features changes.

1. Mark a step from the Live Activity. On the travel day the Lock Screen Live Activity and the expanded Dynamic Island show a button with the traveller's next step in their own words (for example "I'm through security"), plus a "Pass" link that opens the saved boarding pass in the app. The button runs an App Intent (LiveActivityIntent) in the app's process: it records the step on the device and updates the card. It does not open the app and sends nothing to a server by itself. Take-off and landing are shown as a question ("Taken off?") with a "Yes" button. The same button is on the Flights tab's live card.
2. Live card. The countdown is drawn as a split-flap board on the Flights card, the Lock Screen and the Home Screen widget, with the two facts that matter at each stage beside it (terminal and check-in area, gate and seat, seat and baggage belt). A delayed flight shows the printed boarding time crossed out.
3. Offline. Trips now show when the device has no connection: the signed-in session is kept on the device (Clerk's secure-store cache) so the journal is not read as signed out.
4. Photos. HEIC photos picked from the library are converted to JPEG on the device before upload.

To see them: sign in with the review account and add or open a flight departing within the next few hours; the Live Activity starts four hours before departure (a Pro feature, as before). The review account already has trips.

The existing review account and credentials remain valid. Use the email sign-in form and choose the password option if prompted for another method.
