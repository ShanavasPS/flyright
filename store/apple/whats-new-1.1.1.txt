• The app opens on Flights, your journal: your own flight comes first, with its live card at the top on the day you fly, and FlyRight welcomes you by name. Your picture there opens Settings.
• A flight in the air stays in your journal with a live row that counts down to the landing.
• New Updates tab: the friends who are in the air and the postcards they send — with a heart and replies under each one. A request to follow you shows at the top until you answer it.
• The postcards you sent stay under "You" on Updates for as long as your friends can still see them.
• Friends is your circle: whoever is flying gets a live card with their gate and delays, and a follower can be followed back in a tap.
• People who signed in without a name can be found and followed again.
