Set your home base. Pick your city, or add the places you've lived and when, and every trip counts from the home you had then. A move now shows as "Moved to London" instead of one long trip.

Every trip on Flights opens with a photo of where you went. Tap it to see all your trips to that city, and change the photo for all of them or just one — your own trip photos come first. Your friends' trips show the same photos.

FlyRight also opens much faster: the screen you open on is the only one built at launch, and the other tabs get ready the first time you visit them.

The app's performance reports no longer carry trip details, share links or names from the screens you visit.
