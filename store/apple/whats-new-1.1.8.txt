Easier to read with larger text. FlyRight now follows your iPhone's text size up to a comfortable limit, so every screen stays tidy even at the largest accessibility sizes.

Trip pages, your flights list and Travel stats rearrange themselves when text is large, so airport codes, times and flight numbers are never cut off.

Settings, tabs and buttons keep their labels whole with larger text, and the notifications page scrolls instead of hiding its text behind the buttons. A shared World poster looks the same whatever your text size.
