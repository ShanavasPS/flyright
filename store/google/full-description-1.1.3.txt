Keep the flights you have taken, plan the ones ahead, and bring your people along. FlyRight is your free travel journal, with Pro for the months you travel.

Your flights, saved for free
Add past and upcoming flights by flight number, scan a boarding pass, import an airline PDF, or enter a trip yourself. Keep your seat, booking reference, notes, ratings and private journal photos. Explore your routes on the globe, see your travel stats and share a travel poster.

Your people follow for free
Follow friends and family and read their permitted postcards without a Pro subscription. When a traveller has Pro, their followers can receive the live trip updates they choose to share. Each traveller controls the audience: followers, close circle, or only themselves.

Pro when you travel
Pro adds monitoring for your own flights: gate, terminal, delays, baggage belt and inbound aircraft information where available. Keep up with your travel day through alerts and a Lock Screen Live Activity. Publish postcards from your trip for the people following you, and prepare new delay-claim documents and follow-ups.

The choice stays yours
Save a future flight to see what Pro can add to that trip. Browse the available plans or set a separate in-app reminder for two days before departure. Continuing free keeps your flight saved. A reminder never starts a subscription or charges you automatically.

When a flight goes wrong
Manually check a past delay for possible EU261 compensation for free. Pro adds new claim preparation and automated claim-deadline follow-ups. Previously saved claim records remain available. Eligibility and the amount depend on the flight and the circumstances; compensation is not guaranteed.

Start without an account
You can keep flights locally and browse plans while signed out. Sign in to sync your journal, follow people, set a Pro reminder, or purchase and restore Pro. After signing in, return to your selected plan and decide whether to continue.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled in your store account settings. Cancelling renewal keeps Pro active through the paid period. Your saved flight history stays yours afterwards. Live information depends on airline and airport availability.
