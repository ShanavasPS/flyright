FlyRight is the app you open on the day you fly, and the one that stands up for you when the flight goes wrong.

TRAVEL DAY, LIVE

Add a flight with just the flight number, scan your boarding pass, or share the airline's confirmation PDF and every leg imports on its own. From then on FlyRight follows the day for you: check-in opens, gate assigned, boarding, pushback, layover time, landing. A live notification keeps the countdown and the next step in front of you without opening the app.

PEOPLE WHO FLY WITH YOU

Follow friends and family and see their travel day live. No more "did you land?" messages. You choose who sees each trip: everyone following you, your close circle, or only you.

YOUR TRAVEL JOURNAL

Every flight becomes a page: notes, a rating, seat and booking reference, photos. Share a moment from inside a trip with the people following you.

THE WORLD YOU HAVE FLOWN

A map of every route, kilometres flown, records and places, with a poster you can share.

WHEN THE FLIGHT GOES WRONG

Because FlyRight is already tracking your flight, you know the moment a delay or cancellation qualifies under EU passenger rights (Regulation EU 261/2004), and how much: up to 600 EUR per passenger. You get a clear verdict, a ready-to-send claim, and deadline reminders so you never miss a claim window.

WHAT YOU GET

Live flight status with gate, delay and layover tracking
Live notification with the countdown to your next step
Boarding pass scanner and airline PDF import
Follow people and share your travel day
Trip journal with photos and notes
World map, travel stats and shareable posters
Instant EU 261/2004 compensation verdicts and claim guidance
No account needed. Sign in only to keep your travels across devices.

Owed Pro adds compensation claims, early warning when your inbound aircraft is running late, and room for more people to follow you. Everything else is free.
