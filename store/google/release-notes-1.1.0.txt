• The app opens on Home: your travel day, the people you follow who are in the air, and what they have posted.
• Flights is your journal, with a live row that counts down to today's landing.
• Friends is your circle: whoever is flying gets their full live card, gate and delays included.
• Home greets you by name: your picture opens Settings, and the + for a new flight sits across from it.
• A trip's page holds one card: countdown, terminal, check-in, gate, seat and belt.
