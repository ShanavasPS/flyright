Keep your booking with the trip: add flights from a PDF or picture and FlyRight can keep the document on each trip, to open at the airport, even offline. Only you can see it.
Arrival dates read from bookings are right: a later date beside a flight (like "not valid after") is no longer taken as the day it lands, and the flight time on the booking settles it.
Opening a kept document shows it in your PDF viewer.
